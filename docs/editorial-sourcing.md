# Editorial sourcing guidelines

Guelma History is a historical archive. Significant dates, quotations, casualty
figures, names, archaeological claims and disputed interpretations should be
supported by references readers can inspect.

- Prefer primary sources when available: archival documents, contemporary
  records, official heritage inventories, photographs with provenance, and
  digitized historical publications.
- Use scholarly books, peer-reviewed research and recognized museum or university
  publications for context. Record the author, title, publisher and publication
  date when a bare URL is not enough to identify the source.
- Separate documented facts from oral history, interpretation and uncertainty.
  Do not invent citations or treat generated text as a source.
- Include French and Arabic references where possible, and preserve the source's
  original title when translating a citation.
- Keep a short “Sources / المصادر” section at the end of each article.

This is an editorial standard, not a claim that every existing article has been
reviewed or that a structured bibliography field already exists in the database.
